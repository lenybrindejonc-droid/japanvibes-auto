Japanvibes Auto

Japanvibes Auto is a personal tool that helps a creator publish and schedule the videos they made themselves on their own TikTok account, and follow their account statistics.

What it does

  • Log in with TikTok to connect your own account.
  • Review each video before it is posted: preview, edit the caption, choose who can view it, choose interaction settings and disclose commercial content.
  • Post now or schedule the approved video for a later time.
  • See your statistics: followers, likes, and views, likes, comments and shares of your videos.

The app runs on the creator's own computer. Videos, access tokens and statistics are stored locally and are never shared with third parties.
