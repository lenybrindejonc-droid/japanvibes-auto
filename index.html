Japanvibes Auto

Japanvibes Auto is a personal tool used by the creator of the TikTok account @japanvibes180 to publish his own Japan travel videos and photo carousels to TikTok and to view the statistics of his own posts.

It is not offered to the public and only accesses the creator's own TikTok account, through TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API.
