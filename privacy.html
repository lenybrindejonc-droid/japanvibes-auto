Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Japanvibes Auto ("the App") is a personal tool used only by the owner of the TikTok account @japanvibes180.

1. Data accessed

With the owner's authorization through TikTok Login Kit, the App accesses: basic profile information (display name, avatar, open ID), the list of the owner's own videos, and statistics for those videos (views, likes, comments, shares). It also uploads media files chosen by the owner.

2. Use of data

This data is used only to publish the owner's content and display his own statistics. It is never sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

3. Storage

Access tokens and statistics are stored privately by the owner and kept only as long as needed. No data from other TikTok users is collected.

4. Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services permissions.

5. Contact

Questions: via the TikTok account @japanvibes180.
