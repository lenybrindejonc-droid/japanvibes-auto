Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

This policy explains what data Japanvibes Auto ("the app"), operated by Japanvibes, processes.

Data we access

  • Profile information (open ID, display name, username, avatar) to show which account you are posting to.
  • Account statistics (followers, following, likes, number of videos) and your public videos with their view, like, comment and share counts, to display your statistics.
  • Access tokens issued by TikTok after you log in, to publish videos you approved.

How data is stored

All data stays on the computer where you run the app. Tokens are stored in a local file readable only by your user account. We operate no server and do not collect, sell or share your data with anyone.

Retention and deletion

Click "Disconnect" in the app to revoke access and delete the stored token. You can also delete the app's local data folder at any time, and revoke access from TikTok (Settings > Security and permissions > Manage app permissions).
